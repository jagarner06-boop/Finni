Finni · Design system v0.1

A calm bank with a companion

Principles

  1. The bank comes first. Finn adds warmth, and the UI carries the clarity.
  2. One expressive element per screen. That's Finn, the tide fill, the ticks, or the peek. Everything else stays quiet.
  3. Color means something. Cobalt marks actions. Semantic colors appear only as dots, rings, and small text. The only gradient is on the hero card.
  4. Money is sacred. Tabular numerals, never abbreviated, cents set lighter, and every amount links to its source.
  5. Approval is where trust is earned. A plain summary, a deliberate hold to approve, and an explicit outcome either way.

Decisions from your answers

Tabs follow the mockups: Home · Agent · Activity · Settings. Money becomes Home, Chat becomes Agent, and Inbox becomes Activity (nudges and transactions). Goals isn't a tab anymore. It's a pushed route opened from Home. In the code, rename (tabs)/index, money, and inbox to match, and add settings.
The mockups use plush Finn in chat and the nudge card. I kept to the brief instead: flat art in-app, plush only for hero moments.
The mockups show the Agent tab icon as a sparkle on Home and as Finn's head in chat. I standardized on Finn's head. The "Ask your agent…" bar on Home stays, with Finn's head in place of the sparkle.
Approval and step-up auth use the flat happy head with a shield badge.
Avatars at 40pt or smaller and the tab icon don't count toward the one-Finn limit. The ticks and the confident head's sparkle get re-exported in cobalt. The heart-eyes head is for goal completion only.
Still open: there's no flat waving, thinking, or celebrating pose (pose-laptop, pose-laugh-card, and pose-wave stand in). The PNGs aren't transparent, so they're shown with multiply blending here.

Diff rationale
deep #0B2545 → ink #14213D. Matches Finn's back and reads less black on cream. foam #F3FAFF → canvas #FAF8F3. The cool blue-white becomes warm cream, taken from his belly. lagoon, coral, sun, and berry are removed. Lagoon was the active tab and the happy color, and both become cobalt. Coral (the badge) becomes cobalt too, since a badge is "new", not "danger". Sun becomes warning #F5A524 and berry becomes danger #E5484D, both muted. kelp → success. Same hex, renamed for its role. New -ink variants. The base semantic hues fail AA as text on white (success is 2.6:1), so each gets a darker variant for text. moodColor now drives the ring only. happy has no ring, thinking is cobalt, worried is warning, celebrating is success.

Motion · Reanimated 4 + expo-haptics

Anything Finn does uses a spring (damping 14, stiffness 180). The UI uses timing curves only: 150 for micro, 250 for standard, 400 for hero. With Reduce Motion on, springs become 150ms fades, loops are disabled, and haptics stay.
